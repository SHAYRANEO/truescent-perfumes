Our Journey

True Scent Perfumes started as a simple dream — to help everyone find a fragrance that truly matches who they are. Founded by Shayra Neo, we believe that your signature scent should feel natural, beautiful, and uniquely yours.

Our Mission

We want to make premium fragrances accessible to everyone. You do not need to spend a fortune to smell amazing. Quality scents should be for everyone.

Our Promise

  • Authentic and high-quality fragrances
  • Honest pricing — no hidden costs
  • Friendly and personal service
  • Carefully curated selections just for you
